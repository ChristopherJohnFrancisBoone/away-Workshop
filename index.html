What markers look for in Texts and Human Experiences

The PETAL scaffold

PPoint. A topic sentence that makes one arguable claim and names the theme. Start with Gow, not with a character: "Gow reveals…", "Through ___, Gow suggests…"
EEvidence. A short, accurate quote embedded in your sentence, with who says it and when. "When Harry admits that 'we don't look back and we don't look forward', …"
TTechnique. Name the technique precisely and describe what it does to the line. "The balanced parallelism…"
AAnalysis. The most important part. Explain why Gow made this choice, what it reveals about human experience, and how context shapes it. Use strong verbs: reveals, exposes, illuminates, challenges, positions.
LLink. Connect back to the question and your thesis. "Thus, Gow suggests that…"

The ladder from C to A

C · The Reteller

Describes what happens. "Coral is sad because her son died." No quotes or techniques, and treats characters as real people.

B · The Technician

Finds quotes and names techniques, but the analysis stops at "this shows she is sad". Lists features without explaining their effect.

A · The Analyst

Treats the play as a construction by Gow. Fuses technique, context and form, and explains what the choice reveals about human experience.
